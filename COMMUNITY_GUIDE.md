# Community Guide

## Label the work accurately

Use `unofficial fan-made community work`, `community interpretation`, or equivalent language when the context could imply canon. Do not call the engine official or imply endorsement by the show, its creators, networks, studios, or rights holders.

## Keep Xavier recognizable

Use the bundled sheet for identity. Report examples where the snake arm changes side, the eye colors swap, the legs become normally human, the beak becomes a nose or muzzle, the six-nipple torso changes, or the deliberately crude construction becomes polished modern CGI.

## Use assets responsibly

Before publishing, confirm that you may use every supplied image, model, clip, voice, music track, and logo. Do not upload full episodes, long unedited clips, ripped dialogue, scripts, show music, scraped fan art, or the archived contest model to this repository. Original generation or user-owned material is the clean fallback.

## Campaign checklist

- Story and Xavier work without the token mention.
- Xavier appears clearly for most of the runtime.
- The hook lands in the first few seconds.
- The result is meaningfully transformed or originally constructed.
- Muted viewing still communicates the premise and progression.
- No charts, price claims, guaranteed returns, fake contract addresses, copied captions, paid engagement, or foreign watermarks.
- Use at most one secondary cashtag or end card unless an active brief expressly requires otherwise.

## Report a bad example

Open a GitHub issue with the mode, prompt, provider, failed output, what should remain locked, and the smallest correction needed. Do not include private messages or material you lack permission to share.
