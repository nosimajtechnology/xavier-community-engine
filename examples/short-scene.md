# Example: Short Scene

**Request:** Xavier helps a cashier whose register is emotionally checked out.

**Mode:** `SCENE`, 12 seconds.

**Hinge:** `checked out` changes from emotional absence to a literal completed transaction.

1. Hook: a tired cashier stares past an empty register while Xavier approaches.
2. Intervention: Xavier sincerely presents the register to itself as a customer.
3. Literalization: the register prints its own receipt and slides toward the exit.
4. Reversal: the cashier becomes the only item left to scan; Xavier calmly considers the help complete.

Use original `The Sims: Bustin' Out` PS2 screenshots for room/prop density and blocking, with the canonical sheet controlling Xavier. Genesis Frame approval precedes boarding.
