# Example: Episode Mode

**Premise:** Xavier offers to help a town carry its history; public monuments become literal luggage.

- **Board 1 — Hook + Setup:** One monument is detached and offered a handle.
- **Board 2 — Escalation:** Townspeople begin checking buildings as baggage.
- **Board 3 — Major Turn:** The town itself is lifted, exposing an older town underneath.
- **Board 4 — Payoff / Resolution:** Xavier declares the past lighter now that no one can stand on it; the suspended town drifts away.

Each board begins from the exact approved final state of the previous board. The engine generates one board at a time and preserves geography, screen direction, monument damage, Xavier's identity, and the selected original-PS2 render contract.
