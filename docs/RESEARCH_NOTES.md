# Research Notes

## Product conclusions

- Xavier's deliberately crude CGI model is identity, not a defect to modernize.
- Fan drawings, models, edits, fandubs, crossovers, and mixed-media reinterpretation are historically and currently native to the community.
- Original scenes need causal semantic logic and sincere delivery, not random surreal objects.
- PS2 grounding should control the world, camera, lighting, density, effects, and capture feel while the canonical sheet controls Xavier.
- Campaign mechanics remain optional and token-secondary.
