# Repair

Diagnose the failed layer before writing a new prompt.

Priority:

1. character identity
2. anatomy and asymmetry
3. action continuity
4. geography and screen direction
5. rendering fidelity
6. premise causality
7. dialogue density
8. captions or generated text
9. decorative finish

Common narrow repairs:

- Preserve framing/action; restore anatomical-left snake arm.
- Preserve face construction; restore anatomical-right cyan eye and anatomical-left brown eye.
- Preserve the scene; restore the neutral resting expression unless a different emotion was requested.
- Preserve the full body; restore the compact approved head-to-body scale and do not use the face callout as proportion authority.
- Keep Xavier and composition; restore the single backward-hinging knee on both legs.
- Keep pose/geography; remove PBR and rebuild light/materials as period-simple PS2 rendering.
- Preserve the first beats; replace a random ending with a consequence caused by Xavier's own intervention.
- Remove unrequested captions or logos while preserving the scene.
- Preserve composition and identity; restore the selected style adapter's line,
  cel paint, temporal rhythm, grain, and exclusions without changing the action.
- When the Late-Z snake terminus becomes a green hand, remove fingers and
  restore the complete snake head on the anatomical-left forearm only.

## Standard-human-leg repair

Attach both the canonical identity asset and supplemental leg-authority asset. Preserve every passing layer and use:

```text
LOCK:
Xavier's approved head, torso, snake arm, costume, shoes, pose intent, framing, environment, lighting, and rendering.

CHANGE ONLY:
Restore each leg to one thigh, one true knee hinging backward, one lower leg, one ankle, and one normal flat sneaker. Both legs use the same rig. In side or three-quarter view, the knee is the rearward apex behind the hip-to-ankle axis.

DO NOT CHANGE:
Do not add a forward human knee, animal hock, second bend, extra segment, S-curve, bowed/hooked leg, elongated foot, or exaggerated reverse curvature. Do not alter any passing detail.
```

A leg-visible result still showing standard human knees fails repair. Do not approve or expand it. If one layer failed, use `LOCK / CHANGE ONLY / DO NOT CHANGE`. If the entire scene is modern, lock composition and action, then regenerate the environment/render stack under the approved PS2 contract. Do not alter either authority asset.

When a registered adapter is active and the whole render stack is wrong, lock
identity, anatomy, composition, and action, then rebuild the rendering stack
under that adapter rather than the PS2 contract. A route change is explicit:
T2V to R2V for identity/anatomy drift, or R2V to Classic Control when opening
composition and geography must be fixed.
