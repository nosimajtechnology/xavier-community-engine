# Canon and identity

## Authority

Use `../assets/xavier-canonical-reference.png` for character identity and base construction. The approved sheet overrides memory, generic prompts, and environmental game references. Official frames supplied for a task may clarify motion, prop use, or obscured detail; they do not authorize redesign.

## Locked mapping

- Long blond hair with crude, broad clumps and the approved silhouette.
- Dark-brown furred body and deliberately awkward, asymmetric proportions.
- Yellow bird-like beak in place of a human nose or animal muzzle.
- Anatomical right eye: bright cyan-blue. Anatomical left eye: brown.
- Anatomical left forearm and hand: bright-green snake from the elbow downward. The snake is anatomy, not a pet or prop.
- Anatomical right hand: ordinary black-gloved hand.
- Legs: bird-like/backward-hinged joint direction. Never replace with normal forward human knees.
- Six nipples when the torso angle and crop make them visible.
- Preserve the approved loincloth/apron, pendant, shoulder accessory, hair bead/feather detail, and white shoes.
- Preserve crude low-poly planes, simple diffuse texture logic, stiffness, and visual discomfort.

For front-facing views, the snake appears on image-right and the cyan eye on image-left. For back-facing views, the snake appears on image-left. Determine sides anatomically, never from screen position alone.

## Optional prop

The shakashuri is optional and must come from a supplied or verified reference when identity-sensitive. Keep it separate from Xavier's body and snake arm. Do not invent its exact construction from memory.

## Failure gate

Reject or repair normal nose/muzzle, two hands, wrong-side or detached snake, duplicated snake, swapped/symmetric eyes, human knees, missing/extra limbs, generic fantasy species, stereotyped regalia, altered hair, modernized clothing, glossy hero build, detailed PBR fur/hair/cloth, or token/logo additions.
